Coaching for Organizations

The challenge

Even your strongest leaders face moments that test them: adapting to a new organization, scaling into a larger role, navigating a reorg, or leading through the rapid transformation AI is bringing to technical work. In large, matrixed organizations, leaders also need to influence across product, engineering, research, and business functions, often without formal authority. These are skills that training programs and management books rarely develop on their own.

Coaching provides the focused, confidential support that meets leaders where they are, whether they are stepping into something new, working through a difficult chapter, or preparing for what comes next.

Who I work with

  • Senior technical leaders and leadership teams operating in complex, matrixed organizations
  • Engineering, data science, and product leaders scaling through rapid growth
  • High-potential leaders preparing for their next level of responsibility
  • Technical executives who need both strategic range and people leadership depth
  • Organizations investing in the long-term development of their leadership bench

What we work on

Navigating transitions and change

New roles, new organizations, expanded scope, reorganizations. I help leaders navigate these shifts with clarity and confidence, building the habits and perspective the moment demands.

Resilience and decision quality

I help leaders develop the steadiness and discernment to handle difficult conversations, make sound decisions under pressure, and maintain effectiveness through ambiguity and change.

Cross-functional influence

Leading at scale means communicating across audiences and influencing without authority, from individual contributors to executives to cross-functional peers across product, engineering, research, and business functions. I help leaders find their voice and learn to lead through clarity.

Leading through AI transformation

AI is reshaping how technical organizations build, decide, and operate. I help leaders make thoughtful bets under uncertainty, guide teams through change, and maintain trust while expectations and workflows shift.

What I bring

I bring 25 years of firsthand operating experience building and leading engineering and data science teams at Microsoft, Google, YouTube, Apple, Meta, and Instagram. I have hired, managed, reorganized, and scaled teams through every kind of challenge my clients face. That experience is the foundation of everything I do as a coach.

I also bring a long-standing practice as a recognized Zen teacher that helps leaders develop steadiness, self-awareness, and discernment under pressure. My books Buddha's Diet and Buddha's Office explore how these perspectives apply to modern challenges. The result is coaching grounded in real experience, not theory.

Engagement formats

Individual coaching

Twice-monthly one-on-one sessions, with support between meetings, focused on the leader's specific challenges and growth.

Group cohorts

Confidential peer groups of 5 to 7 leaders meeting twice a month for shared reflection on leadership, complexity, and decision-making.

Combined

Individual coaching paired with group sessions, combining focused personal support with shared perspective across the team.

Experience

I've coached and advised leaders at companies including Lyft, Apple, DeepMind, and YouTube, as well as many earlier-stage startups across AI, health tech, and consumer products. I've supported leaders through promotion, scope expansion, reorganization, team conflict, and executive transition.

I've also led workshops and retreats for teams at Uber, Apple, Instagram, and LinkedIn, and I'm a faculty member at the Esalen Institute in Big Sur.

How we begin

We usually begin with a conversation to understand your goals, identify the right leaders to support, and design an engagement that fits your context. We can start with a single leader or a small cohort, and expand from there.

Engagements are typically structured as a six-month commitment, though shorter timelines are possible. There is no commitment required to have an initial conversation.
