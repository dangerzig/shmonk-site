Teams & Organizations

I work with organizations through executive coaching, technical advising, and talks, workshops, and retreats for teams. All draw on 25 years of leadership experience at companies including Apple, Microsoft, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and Google.

Executive coaching

Many organizations bring me on to coach key leaders – whether they're navigating a critical transition, stepping into a bigger role, or leading through a period of rapid change. This is the same deep, one-on-one work I do with individual clients, but sponsored by the organization and shaped to its context.

I help leaders develop the clarity and steadiness to make better decisions, communicate more effectively, and sustain high performance in a way that's actually sustainable.

As an outside coach informed by decades inside organizations ranging from Fortune 100 companies to early-stage startups – and extensive experience with non-profits as well as for-profits – I bring a perspective that only an outsider with deep insider experience can offer. An outside relationship also creates a level of trust and candor that can be hard to establish with someone inside the organization, giving leaders a genuinely confidential space to think out loud.

In addition to one-on-one coaching, I also work with small leadership cohorts, peer groups who meet regularly for reflection and conversation around shared challenges.

Engagements typically begin with a scoping conversation to understand the organization's goals and identify the right leaders to work with. From there, coaching follows a six-month cadence of twice-monthly sessions, with communication in between as needed. That's a typical structure, but I'm open to other models – some clients prefer to meet every week. I'm happy to provide periodic updates to the sponsoring stakeholder, while keeping the content of individual sessions confidential.

Background

I spent 25 years building and leading teams at Apple, Microsoft, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and Google. I sit on the boards of both for-profit companies and non-profit organizations, and I'm a trustee of Naropa University. I've coached and advised leaders across technology, finance, healthcare, and the non-profit sector.

I'm also an author (Buddha's Office, Buddha's Diet), a trained meditation teacher, and a faculty member at the Esalen Institute. This combination of hands-on leadership experience and contemplative training gives me a different lens on the challenges leaders face – one grounded in both the realities of organizational life and a deeper attention to how people actually think, communicate, and make decisions.

Technical advising

I also take on a limited number of technical advisory engagements, drawing on my experience building and scaling data science and engineering organizations. Areas of focus include data science and AI strategy, AI transformation, and scaling technical organizations and their leaders.

These engagements vary in shape. Sometimes it's ongoing advisory work with a founder or CTO; sometimes it's a focused engagement around a specific challenge or transition. If you're facing a technical leadership question and think an outside perspective might help, I'm happy to talk it through.

Talks, workshops, and retreats

I also work with organizations through talks, retreats, and both onsite and offsite programs. These are shaped to the context and the people in the room, and may include teaching, conversation, open Q&A, or guided practices that are welcoming and accessible, including for those with no prior experience.

Topics

  • Leading through complexity – Building the capacity to stay grounded and make clear decisions when the pace is relentless and the stakes are high.
  • Culture and alignment – Creating the conditions for honest dialogue, shared purpose, and the kind of trust that retains senior talent.
  • Navigating transitions – Reorganizations, leadership changes, strategic pivots. Helping teams move through uncertainty with clarity about what's ending, what's emerging, and what needs to be carried forward.

Formats

Talks

Single-session keynotes or fireside conversations, 45–90 minutes. Ideal for team meetings, offsites, or conferences.

Workshops

Half-day or full-day sessions combining teaching, guided practice, and group dialogue.

Retreats

Multi-day offsites for deeper exploration, team connection, and renewal.
